ESCAPEMENT

a cultivation RPG — in development in the Blue Mountains, NSW

I'm Jake, a solo developer in the Blue Mountains, NSW, building Escapement — a cultivation RPG (the genre from Chinese fantasy: martial artists refining body and spirit to transcend death) set in a world of water clocks, immortals, and cockatoos. Fate has long since shackled you with its decree: you are going to die young. What will you do with the little time you have left?

The game

Fate is not a metaphor here: the heavens have measured out every day of your life, including your last. You are the cog that won't keep time; each death winds the world back to day one. Jam the works.

Escapement is a time-loop RPG crossed with a cultivation life-sim. You are a mortal disciple mastering the same scheduled life, and the death that ends it, one repetition at a time. Where other loops give you minutes or a day, this one gives you a lifetime: room to learn a craft, turn a stranger into a sworn friend, and still die on schedule.

  • The world runs on a fixed calendar, and so does everyone in it. Every lecture, festival, feud, and disaster falls on schedule until you intervene; every character keeps a real daily routine, life after life. Learning the calendar and its people is the progression.
  • Knowledge is the progression that survives death. Routes, people, recipes, techniques: your real save file is what you've learned.
  • Each "Fated Death" is a boss fight you are fated to lose. Beat it anyway: uncover one of the many hidden advantages that tilt it, or come back strong enough to win it head-on.
  • The world is a water-driven machine inside a living Blue Mountains biosphere: gum forest and sandstone country, lyrebirds, funnel-webs, and kangaroos. Qi flows through the land's veins, and Australia's famously deadly wildlife cultivates right alongside you.

Explore the mountains. Dig up their secrets. Befriend disciples, rivals, and elders. Temper your body. Spar your allies. Meditate and gather qi. Brew pills in a borrowed cauldron. Fight the local wildlife. Meet your fated death on the day it was written, and wake on day one with a better plan. Again. And again. Until the works jam.

Now hiring — Australia-based game artists

Applications open · paid concept test · $50/hr AUD · any style · remote

I'm commissioning paid concept tests from a small shortlist of artists working in deliberately different styles — the game's art style gets chosen by choosing its artists.

For calibration, the art I love: Ori and the Blind Forest, Hades, Sea of Stars, Disco Elysium. Shortlisted artists get the full taste brief (what I love and why) and world brief.

The test — a paid concept commission

  • Three quick, rough concept sketches: two named characters and a creature.
  • Rough, uncoloured sketches — concept work, not finished illustration.
  • $150 AUD fixed — $75 on signing, $75 on delivery (when the IP transfers).
  • Scoped to roughly 2–3 hours of work · ~5-day window · one revision round included.
  • A short plain-English contract signed first: IP assigns on final payment; you keep portfolio rights once the game announces.

The gig it leads to

  • Roughly $15,000 AUD in the first funded round — 300 hours at $50/hr: character portraits, location scenes, key art, and an icon set.
  • That first round isn't the whole plan — as more funding lands, more art passes follow, and I'd rather keep working with artists I already trust.
  • One artist doing everything, or the budget split across specialists — character, environment, concept, icon. Generalists and specialists equally welcome.
  • Strong applicants I don't select now stay on file (with your permission) for later roles.
  • Contract work from around the end of 2026. I'm applying to Screen Australia's Emerging Gamemakers Fund this round; if that misses, I'll be pursuing state programs and other funding paths — I will find the funding one way or another.

Requirements

  • Australia-based (a funding requirement — the money must be spent in Australia).
  • A portfolio of strong work in any style. 2D game or illustration work is a plus, and excellent 3D portfolios are welcome too. Tell me your specialty if you have one.
  • Comfortable signing IP assignment and a warranty that delivered work is your own hand (no generative AI in deliverables).

The process

Application → video-call interview with strong applicants → a paid concept test (~5-day window) → strong results lead to further paid work now, and the funded engagement when the money lands.

Applications are reviewed as they arrive until the roles are filled. Interviewed artists hear back within a week of the call.
